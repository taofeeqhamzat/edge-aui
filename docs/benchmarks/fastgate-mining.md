## What the case names mean

- `live_pre_fix`: Audit corpus shape (9 sequences × 18 symbols, support 2) at the bounds the old gate effectively applied: unlimited length and count.
- `bounded_production`: Participant-shaped corpus (35 sessions × 18 symbols, support 2) at the production bounds: length 4, 32 patterns.
- `bounded_evidence_floor`: Same corpus with support 3, the participant evidence floor used by the declared set.
- `bounded_worst_corpus`: Largest corpus the runtime will send (maxCorpusSequences 40) with long 60-symbol sequences, production bounds.

## Reading the result

`live_pre_fix` shows the cost the F-02 fix removes: the same corpus under an effectively
unbounded pattern length and count returns two orders of magnitude more patterns than the
production bounds. `bounded_production` is the cost a session actually pays. Neither
number supports a claim about the 50 ms interaction budget: the mining call runs inside
the worker, off the main thread, and the browser budget is not measured here.
